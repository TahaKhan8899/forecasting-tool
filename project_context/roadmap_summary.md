# Roadmap Summary

1.  **Data Automation & Preparation:** Automate core metric calculations (AOV, repeat rates, etc.), establish robust data pipelines from Shopify (multi-store), validate data.
2.  **Integration of Ad Spend Data:** Connect to Meta, Google, Amazon, TikTok Ads APIs. Consolidate 24 months historical ad spend. Ensure ongoing data sync.
3.  **Historical Analysis (aMER):** Calculate historical aMER (Ad Spend / New Customer Revenue). Analyze correlations and seasonality.
4.  **AI Forecasting Layer Development:** Build initial AI forecasting models for spend and new customer revenue. Explore CLV prediction.
5.  **Advanced Profitability Modeling:** Integrate COGS, margins, fixed costs. Enable comprehensive "what-if" scenario analysis.
6.  **Product Interface & Visualization:** Develop interactive dashboards for historical data, forecasts, and scenario comparisons.
